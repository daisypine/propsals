Phase 3 + 4

On-Page SEO &
Local Domination

Every Page Framework
  • Service pages: 1,000+ words. Local cost context, care details, strong CTA
  • Location hubs: 600–800 words. Neighborhood-aware, local facilities Katie knows
  • Inner pages: 600–800 words. "[Care type] [city]" — high intent, lower competition
  • Schema markup everywhere: LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Person, Review
Google Business Profile + Citations
  • Full GBP audit + optimization — categories, services, photos, Q&A
  • Tight 10-mile service area — Google rewards specificity over broad claims
  • NAP consistency: Yelp, Bing, Apple Maps, Caring.com, A Place for Mom, SeniorAdvisor
  • Review generation system — past families are an untapped source of 5-star reviews
